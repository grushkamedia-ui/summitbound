California · 2026

Getting licensed in California

The steps for a life-only license, in order. We walk you through each one.

  1. 12-hour course

    Complete the 12-hour ethics and California Insurance Code course.

  2. State exam

    Pass the state life-only exam.

  3. Apply for your license

    Apply with the California Department of Insurance, including fingerprints.

Since January 1, 2026, the 20-hour prelicensing course is no longer required. Check the current steps at insurance.ca.gov.

How we support new agents

15 in 100still with their hiring company after 4 years

Most people who start in this industry don't stay with the company that hired them. LIMRA found that in 2020, only about 15 in 100 new financial professionals at agencies that hire beginners were still with their hiring company after four years. Their research points to what helps people stay: early activity, sales training and mentoring.

Ask us too

Five questions to ask any insurance recruiter

  1. Am I an employee or an independent contractor?
  2. What will I pay for, like leads, licensing and tools?
  3. Who trains me, and what does week one look like?
  4. What does a typical new agent actually earn? Ask for the typical result, not the top one.
  5. What happens if I want to leave?
